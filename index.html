Part 1: How to Sell an Idea to the "Lizard Brain"

Why pitching with pure logic fails—and how to get past the brain's survival filter.

1. The Lizard Brain Gate Open

Reptilian / Amygdala (The Bouncer)

Fast, survival-driven, and hates burning energy. Asks: "Is it safe? Is it boring? Is it too complex?"

⚡ Reaction: Safe, visual & urgent — passes idea up!
2. The Midbrain Engaged

Limbic System (Emotion & Status)

Processes gut feelings, social belonging, and personal value: "How does this make me feel?"

❤️ Reaction: "I feel the benefit — I want this."
3. The Neocortex Buy-In ("Yes!")

Logical Brain (The Justifier)

Slow and analytical. Once the Lizard Brain approves, the Neocortex uses logic to justify saying "Yes".

🧠 Reaction: Rationalizes the decision and agrees!

6 Rules for Pitching an Idea to the Lizard Brain:

1. "What's In It For Me?"

The Lizard Brain is 100% self-centered. Start with their pain or problem, not your company history.

2. Sharp Contrast (Before/After)

Abstract numbers don't register. Show a clear contrast: Pain Now vs. Relief With Your Idea.

3. Keep It Simple & Concrete

Avoid complex jargon. If understanding your idea burns too many mental calories, the Lizard Brain shuts down.

4. Visuals & Short Stories

The visual nerve reaches the old brain much faster than words. Paint a picture they can see.

5. Loss Aversion (Pain > Gain)

People hate losing 2x more than they love gaining. Highlight what they lose by doing nothing.

6. Lower the Threat

High pressure triggers fight-or-flight pushback. Make the next step feel safe and low-risk.

Vocabulary Spotlight

What Does "Leverage" Mean? (Physics vs. Business & Trading)

How the physical machine (a lever on a fulcrum) gave birth to the financial & business vocabulary.

Leverage Ratio: 5x (5:1)
⚙️ 1. In Physics (The Machine)

Effort (Input): 100 N force

Fulcrum: Pivot point

Mechanical Advantage: 5x arm ratio

Load (Output): 500 N lifted

📈 2. In Trading & Business

Margin / Equity: $1,000 your cash

Financial Fulcrum: $4,000 borrowed

Leverage Ratio: 5:1 (5x)

Market Exposure: $5,000 position
