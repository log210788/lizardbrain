The "Crocodile / Lizard Brain" 3-Step Pitch & "Leverage" Vocabulary

How the 3-Step Executive Pitch Framework gets past the primitive brain, plus how Physics Leverage relates to Business & Trading Leverage.

Part 1: The 3-Step "Crocodile Brain" Pitch Framework

How each step of the script is engineered to wake up the primitive brain without triggering resistance.

Step 1: The Macro Shift 🦎 Wakes Up

"Over the past [6-18 months], the market / operational landscape in [industry] has shifted..."

Crocodile Brain Effect: A crocodile ignores static things, but movement in its environment triggers instant attention—with zero personal threat.
Step 2: High-Stakes Friction ⚡ Survival / Pain

"Yet, most organizations are still relying on legacy approaches, leading to [cost / bottleneck]..."

Crocodile Brain Effect: "Yet..." creates sharp contrast. Blaming "legacy approaches" protects their ego while triggering loss aversion (inaction = pain).
Step 3: The Value Anchor 🧠 Unlocks Logic

"Our objective today is to explore how [our solution] eliminates this friction and delivers [specific, quantified outcome]."

Crocodile Brain Effect: "Explore how" lowers pressure (safe!), promises relief from friction, and hands the Logical Brain 1 tangible number.

Key Vocabulary from the 3-Step Pitch:

Macro shift

A big-picture, industry-wide change in the environment (vs. micro details).

Operational landscape

The overall environment and day-to-day reality of how businesses operate.

Legacy approaches

Old, outdated methods or technology still used out of habit.

High-stakes friction / Bottleneck

Serious obstacles or congestion points that slow down a system and waste money.

Value anchor

Setting a clear, high-value reference point in the listener's mind right at the start.

Quantified outcome

A result measured with a specific number (%, $, or hours saved) rather than a vague claim.

What Does "Leverage" Mean? (Physics vs. Business & Trading)

How the physical machine (a lever on a fulcrum) gave birth to the financial & business vocabulary.

Leverage Ratio: 5x (5:1)
⚙️ 1. In Physics (The Machine)

Effort (Input): 100 N force

Fulcrum: Pivot point

Mechanical Advantage: 5x arm ratio

Load (Output): 500 N lifted

📈 2. In Trading & Business

Margin / Equity: $1,000 your cash

Financial Fulcrum: $4,000 borrowed

Leverage Ratio: 5:1 (5x)

Market Exposure: $5,000 position
